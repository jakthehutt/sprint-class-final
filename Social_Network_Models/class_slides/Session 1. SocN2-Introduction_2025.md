Reading and writing advice
DEPARTMENT OF NETWORK AND DATA SCIENCE (DNDS)
•
Cultivate reading and writing as a habit, find a place 
and time for it each day, avoid distractions, try to read 
on paper not digitally, print and buy books used or 
borrow them in the library, take notes, don’t read 
word-by-word
•
Write down the overall structure and key points of the 
text after you read it, write down any thoughts / 
associations on other material that you encountered 
during your study, write by hand, don’t use a laptop or 
tablet, talk to others about your readings

A note on the history of network science
DEPARTMENT OF NETWORK AND DATA SCIENCE (DNDS)
•
Harvard school (Harrison White, Block 
models) and California school (Freeman, 
centrality measures) in the 1970s
•
Cultural turn in sociological network analysis 
(Breiger) and blossoming of structuralist 
approaches (e.g., Burt) in 1980-1990s
•
From 2000s onward: physicists and 
computer scientists start network science
