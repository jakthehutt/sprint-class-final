Instructions for writing the term paper. 
 
Writing style:  
- 
Clear sentences – pay attention to the structure of the text  
- 
Paragraphs: one thought = one idea; use subsections in italic  
- 
Follow journal style structure: Introduction. Literature background, Theory. Methods. 
Data, Measures, Results, Discussion  
- 
Avoid passive and relative terms (could, might, maybe, etc.) 
- 
References, substantiate your claims and analytical decisions  
 
Presentation of results and methods:  
- 
Never use screenshots, always provide proper tables/figures  
- 
Labels in figures should be the same size as the main text  
- 
Never describe variable names /how you loaded and manipulated the dataset, avoid 
technical details  
- 
Avoid repetitions and separate clearly between results and methods sections.  
- 
In results section: avoid post-hoc reasoning. Just describe the results. 
 
Embedding in previous literature: 
- 
In Introduction: what is the contribution of your study?  
- 
Literature BG: what has been done so far?  
- 
Methods/Measures: substantiate your analytical strategy  
- 
Results: overlap/contrast with previous work?  
- 
Discussion: what to do next? Target audience  
 
Theory/ expectations:  
- 
Where do your theoretical expectations come from? Derive Hypotheses based on past 
work.  
- 
Why do you expect certain patterns in the data?  
 
In general: 
- 
Try to stay close to journal articles in tone, structure, and presentation of results  
  ⇒ Take one article as a blueprint for your own study and emulate this article
