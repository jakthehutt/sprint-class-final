# Project Guidelines: Statistical Network Models (SocN2)

Based on the instructions from `Instructions for writing.md` and `Session 1. SocN2-Introduction_2025.md`, the following guidelines must be followed for the final project report:

## 1. Formal Requirements
*   **Format:** Research report.
*   **Length:** Approximately 3,500 - 5,000 words (excluding tables and figures).
*   **Group Size:** Up to three students.
*   **Deadline:** April 7.
*   **Grading Weight:** 65% for the research report (plus 20% for a 15-20 min presentation and 15% for seminar attendance/participation).

## 2. Structure of the Paper
Follow a standard journal article structure. Use italicized subsections and ensure each paragraph focuses on one specific idea. 
1.  **Introduction:** Clearly outline the contribution of the study.
2.  **Literature Background:** Describe what has been done so far.
3.  **Theory:**
    *   Where do theoretical expectations come from?
    *   Derive hypotheses strictly based on past work.
    *   Explain *why* certain patterns in the data are expected.
4.  **Methods:**
    *   Substantiate the analytical strategy chosen.
5.  **Data & Measures:**
    *   Never describe variable names or technical details on how you loaded and manipulated the dataset.
6.  **Results:**
    *   Just describe the results directly. Avoid post-hoc reasoning.
    *   Separate clearly from the methods section (avoid repetitions).
    *   Identify overlaps or contrasts with previous work.
7.  **Discussion:**
    *   What should be done next?
    *   Target audience implications.

## 3. Writing Style and Presentation
*   **Tone:** Try to stay close to journal articles in tone, structure, and presentation of results. It is highly recommended to take one specific article as a "blueprint" and emulate it.
*   **Clarity:** Write clear sentences, paying close attention to text structure.
*   **Phrasing:** Avoid passive and relative terms (e.g., *could*, *might*, *maybe*).
*   **References:** Substantiate all claims and analytical decisions with proper references.

## 4. Figures and Tables
*   **No Screenshots:** Never use screenshots. Always provide proper, self-generated tables and figures.
*   **Label Sizing:** Labels inside figures must be the same size as the main text in the document.

## 5. Theoretical Approach
*   Be conscious of the role of theory in social network analysis (e.g., referencing Rawlings et al., 2023).
*   Formulate hypotheses logically and link analytical approaches back to sociological paradigms (formalist vs. relational accounts).
